# Implementation notes for Supplementary Table S1

For the 15-row runner/output map see [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md), and for exact commands and filenames see [README.md](README.md).

## Source and QC choices

The ROI is 14–28° N, 160–200° E. ERSSTv6 inputs are native 2° monthly NOAA fields for 1998–2024, with cos(latitude) weighting and calendar-month-specific median outlier removal before annual means. Its trend map fits OLS on native grid cells. Regional satellite CSVs have already undergone spatial averaging, so the original chlorophyll grid slopes cannot be reconstructed from them.

The BGC runner selects the manuscript years 2016, 2017, 2020–2024. Each of adjusted CHLA, BBP700 and DOXY has independent finite-value/profile/month selection, but **all three use `CHLA_ADJUSTED_QC==1` as a shared row gate**. This does not verify DOXY or BBP's own adjusted QC. Each accepted BGC profile requires ≥5 valid observations in 0–300 dbar; each platform-year requires ≥10 distinct months and trajectory intersection with the ROI. AOU is `gsw_O2sol(SA,CT,p,lon,lat)-DOXY_ADJUSTED`, with >1000 adjusted oxygen ×0.1 and the range/platform checks in `npsg_aou_gsw`. No raw DOXY fallback occurs. The 1 m annual AOU field uses platform monthly medians, equal platform monthly averages and ≥3 months per depth.

Physical Argo uses adjusted pressure, salinity and temperature after both salinity and temperature adjusted QC flags equal 1. Pressure's own QC is unverified. It restricts 2003–2024, January–November, 0–300 dbar, ≥10 unique pressure levels/profile, and the ROI. Profiles used for MLD must span ≤5 to ≥290 m with no >50 m vertical gap. T/S spikes are removed using a seven-level moving-median threshold factor of 5, and SA must be 30–41 g kg⁻¹. GSW converts SP/t/p to SA/CT/sigma0 and geometric depth; CT/SA are interpolated only within observed depths and smoothed over five 1 m cells before sigma0 is recalculated. MLD is the first 1 m cell with sigma0≥sigma0(10 m)+0.03 kg m⁻³ and requires a valid matching monthly satellite Zeu. Monthly MLD is the mean of accepted profile values; the other annual fields use platform monthly medians, equal represented months (minimum six/platform/year/depth) and equal platforms. Selected isopycnal targets are explicit parameters in the example.

`npsg_layer_mean_ohc` reproduces the original physical script's **mean volumetric** OHC metric in GJ m⁻³, allocating each supported 1 m grid cell's thickness to 0–MLD, MLD–supplied Zeu, and Zeu–300 m by exact overlap. It reports supported thickness; this means a partially observed layer is an average over its supported portion. `npsg_layer_heat` remains an optional complete-support J m⁻² integral helper and is **not** the default figure metric. The annual depth OHC field is J m⁻³ and its anomaly uses each depth's mean over available years.

Monthly optical `Date` strings are **day/month/year** (for example `1/2/1998` = February 1998). `Zeu_from_Kd490` near 104 m is separate from the calculated 1% depth near 162 m. The CbPM breakpoint ratio uses its existing `chl_monthly`, matched by month with bbp443. `npsg_screen_daily` uses 61 observations and ≥15 local observations. For monthly breakpoint values, the runner uses within-year median outlier threshold factor 3 and January–November to align with physical MLD.

The breakpoint algorithm tests every split leaving ≥5 annual values each side, fits two OLS segments, and uses 1000 residual-bootstrap replicates under a single-trend null for the maximum F and slope-change statistics. It compares AIC, AICc and BIC with ΔIC = IC(single) − IC(break). `Supported` follows the original physical script and Table S6: **sup-F p<0.05 OR ΔBIC≥2**. Davies-type p, AIC, AICc and continuous segmented regression are supplementary diagnostics. `SupportedStrictDiagnostic` reports the superseded all-three rule for comparison only. The Table S1 breakpoint row should be revised accordingly. Full figure confidence intervals are not generated. The manuscript's printed `12128,b_{bp,470}` is interpreted as multiplication (`12128*b_{bp,470}`), as shown by the carbon calibration.

Output files do not imply that all manuscript figures have been reproduced. See [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md) for missing gridded chlorophyll and HOT inputs.
